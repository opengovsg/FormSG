# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Government agency staff who build digital forms and multi-step workflows for citizens. They range from tech-savvy product managers to non-technical policy officers. Their context: replacing paper processes under time pressure, often without design support. They need to feel guided, not overwhelmed.

Citizens and businesses fill out the published forms. They expect clarity, speed, and trust that their data is handled properly.

## Product Purpose

FormSG lets Singapore government agencies digitize paper-based processes into online forms and approval workflows. Success looks like: a non-technical officer can build, configure, and launch a multi-step workflow without help desk support, and a citizen can complete it without confusion.

## Brand Personality

**Friendly, Helpful, Clear.**

Warm but competent. Guides users through complexity with encouragement, not hand-holding. Speaks plainly. Avoids jargon. Celebrates completion without being performative. The tone of a knowledgeable colleague, not a corporate chatbot or a kindergarten teacher.

## Anti-references

- **Cold enterprise tools** (Salesforce, ServiceNow): Gray, dense, intimidating. FormSG should never feel like it requires training to use.
- **Over-designed SaaS** (gratuitous animations, gradient buttons, startup-cute illustrations): Decoration without purpose. Motion and delight should earn their place.
- **Generic government portals** (dated gov.sg patterns): Bureaucratic, visually stale. FormSG represents what modern government digital services can look like.

## Design Principles

1. **Guide, don't gate.** Progressive disclosure over locked steps. Show the path forward, let users move at their own pace.
2. **Plain language first.** If a label needs explanation, rewrite the label. Technical accuracy matters, but clarity wins.
3. **Earn every element.** No decoration without function. Every animation, color, and component should help the user complete their task.
4. **Trust through consistency.** Same patterns, same vocabulary, same behavior across every screen. Predictability builds confidence.
5. **Respect the officer's time.** Government staff are busy. Reduce clicks, pre-fill what you can, make the common path fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance as baseline
- 4.5:1 minimum contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text
- Full keyboard navigation support
- Screen reader compatibility
- Reduced motion alternatives for all animations
- Color-blind safe semantic states (don't rely on color alone for error/success/warning)

## Surface Notes

The public landing page is a **brand** surface (design IS the product). Everything behind login is **product** (design SERVES the product). Default register is product; brand register applies only to the public-facing homepage.

## References

- **Typeform**: Conversational flow, delightful micro-interactions, clean focused experience. The feeling of being guided through something complex without noticing the complexity.
- **Notion**: Friendly onboarding, flexible block-based building, approachable complexity. The feeling of capability without intimidation.
