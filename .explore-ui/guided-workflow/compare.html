Claude's Ranking

1 Stacked Scroll Recommended
Simple vertical stacking. Completed sections stay visible and editable. No visual tricks, just clean progression.
Closest to existing FormSG patterns. Low cognitive load. Nothing to learn.
Less visual emphasis on the current section. Could feel flat for longer steps.
2 Focus Fade
Active section at full opacity, completed sections dim to 50%. Draws attention to what needs action while keeping context.
Strong visual focus on the current task. Makes progress feel tangible.
Dimmed sections might feel "locked" to some users. Clicking to restore adds interaction complexity.
3 Conversational
Friendly transition messages between sections ("Nice! Now let's..."). More onboarding-like, warmer tone.
Most approachable for first-time users. Builds confidence through encouragement.
Transition messages add vertical space. May feel patronizing for experienced users. Harder to maintain consistent tone.
