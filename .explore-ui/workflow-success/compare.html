Claude's Rankings

1
Contextual Overlay
Recommended
Minimal modal over dimmed workflow. User sees their work behind the celebration. Quick to dismiss.
+ Hits excitement + reassurance. They see what they built AND get celebrated.
- Lightest touch of the three. Might not feel like enough of a "moment" for some.
2
Flow Takeover
Full panel replacement with visual flow diagram showing all steps connected. Biggest emotional impact.
+ Strongest "moment". The flow diagram gives a bird's-eye view they haven't seen. Most empowering.
- More to build. Diagram needs to reflect actual step data. Slightly heavier UX.
3
Summary Modal
Classic modal with checkmark and bullet list summarizing what was configured. Clear and informative.
+ Informative. Confirms everything is set up. Familiar pattern.
- The bullet summary is slightly redundant since they just configured everything. Less emotional.
