# FormSG

Singapore government form builder. Forms are created by agency admins and filled by respondents, with answers delivered through several response modes.

## Language

**Multirespondent form (MRF)**:
A form whose single submission is filled across multiple workflow steps by different respondents (`FormResponseMode.Multirespondent`).
_Avoid_: Unified mode, unified form — product shorthand that does not appear in code.

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Unified mode" was used to mean **Multirespondent form (MRF)** — resolved: they are synonyms; prefer **MRF** since that is the term the code uses.
